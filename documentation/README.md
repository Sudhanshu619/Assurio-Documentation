## A reviewable decision has six parts

1. **Identified configuration** — the exact system and component baseline.
2. **Declared purpose** — who uses it, why, where and with what exclusions.
3. **Assurance requirements** — measurable claims and critical controls.
4. **Evaluations** — results linked to scenarios, methods and operating domains.
5. **Evidence review** — both supporting and adverse findings visible to reviewers.
6. **Scoped decision** — permitted operations, conditions, accountable person and reconsideration triggers.

A strong outcome is not simply a green dashboard. It is an explanation a future reviewer can reconstruct: *What was approved, on what basis, by whom, and what would force reconsideration?*

## Publication note

Assurio's publicly described capabilities form the basis of this documentation. An organization may implement specific approval gates, score thresholds, role names, connectors and retention rules differently. Apply your local policies and validate UI paths in your deployment.
