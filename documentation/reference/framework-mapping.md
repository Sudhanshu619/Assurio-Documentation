# Framework and policy mapping

> Use control references without overstating compliance

Assurio's public material mentions mappings to the **NIST AI Risk Management Framework**, **ISO/IEC 42001**, **ISO/IEC 23894**, the **EU AI Act**, and internal controls. These frameworks and regulations have different scopes, legal status and assessment methods. A mapping is **not** a determination of legal compliance or certification.

## Mapping method

1. Establish which external provisions and internal policies apply to the system, jurisdiction and organization.
2. Maintain the authoritative framework/version reference and responsible compliance owner.
3. Translate each applicable obligation or management objective into operational controls.
4. Link controls to risks, evidence requirements, actual artifacts, exception decisions and monitoring obligations.
5. Review unmapped clauses and identify when a control addresses only part of an obligation.
6. Escalate legal interpretations to qualified legal/compliance professionals.
7. Reassess mappings when the source framework, law, system or operating context changes.

## Practical distinction

A risk assessment may support a risk-management obligation but does not establish that required transparency, governance, documentation, conformity or post-market duties are all met. Make each scope limitation explicit.
