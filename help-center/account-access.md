# Account and access

> Sign-in and account-readiness diagnostics

The public product information does not specify Assurio's production login URL, signup model or supported SSO providers. Your administrator should supply the correct environment address and account-provisioning procedure.

## I cannot sign in

1. Confirm the URL and environment are the approved ones for your organization.
2. Confirm your user identity has been provisioned and is assigned to the required tenant/organization.
3. Check whether your organization's SSO/MFA policy requires an additional step.
4. Check for expired invitations, revoked access or a role change.
5. Record the error text, timestamp, browser and whether other colleagues are affected.
6. Contact your organization administrator or official support path, without sharing passwords or tokens.

## I signed in but see no systems

You may have no assigned scope, insufficient visibility or an incorrect environment. Ask an administrator to verify membership, attribute-based rules and the intended AI system ID rather than creating a duplicate record.

## Security reminder

Do not bypass organizational authentication policy or send screenshots that expose customer data or secrets. The exact account-reset steps should be added only after validating the real sign-in interface.
