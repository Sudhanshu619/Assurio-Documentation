# Frequently asked questions

> Answers about AI governance, risk, TEVV and Assurio

### What is Assurio?

Assurio is an AI governance, risk and assurance platform connecting AI inventory, system governance, evaluation, evidence, findings, approval decisions and change reviews across models, agents and autonomous systems.

### Is it only a model-testing tool?

No. Testing is part of the lifecycle, but governance and assurance also require risk context, traceability, quality review, accountability and decisions tied to a precise operating scope.

### Does Assurio certify an AI system or guarantee compliance?

No. The public product information expressly avoids claims of universal proof of AI safety, automatic certification or authority to operate. Compliance depends on the applicable obligations and organizational practices.

### Can I govern AI agents?

Assurio describes agent identity, tool permissions, action boundaries, escalation, human oversight and runtime-control concepts. The actual enforcement design depends on your system and integration architecture.

### Can it evaluate adaptive or autonomous systems?

Its assurance model supports configuration baselines, multi-environment TEVV, human decisions and revalidation after change. Do not assume any particular simulation platform is natively integrated without confirmation.

### What is the difference between evidence and approval?

Evidence documents what was observed under specific conditions. Approval is a named human authority's determination about whether a particular configuration may operate, given the full evidence and residual risk.

### Does framework mapping make us compliant with the EU AI Act or ISO certified?

No. Mapping helps connect controls and evidence to framework provisions but cannot replace legal or conformity assessments.

### How does Assurio respond when a model changes?

The described lifecycle connects changed components to evidence and decisions so affected claims can be reassessed. Actual trigger integrations and enforcement rules must be configured and verified.
