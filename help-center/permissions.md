# Permissions and approval questions

> Diagnose authorization issues and decision-rights gaps

## Why can't I view a system or evidence item?

Access may depend on tenant scope, role, data classification, project context, or attribute-based rules. Verify the object ID and your assigned scope with the administrator. Access to a system summary does not necessarily mean permission to download raw evidence.

## Why can't I approve a decision?

Acceptance authority is an organizational responsibility, not simply an editing capability. Even if the interface exposes a decision action, the user must hold delegated authority for that use, risk tier and operating environment.

## Why is a reviewer asking for independent review?

Where separation of duties applies, the person who generated evidence may not be the sole approver. The additional reviewer challenges the completeness and applicability of the evidence set, especially when there are safety, privacy or high-impact risks.

## Escalation checklist

Provide system/baseline ID, desired action, exact object, assigned role, business justification and an administrator-approved ticket reference. Never request broad administrator privileges when a narrower permission is sufficient.
