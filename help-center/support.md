# Contact support

> Prepare a useful support request without exposing sensitive information

For official product inquiries or an Assurio demonstration, begin with [Assurio at SDLC Corp](https://sdlccorp.com/products/assurio/) and its request-a-demo/contact links. A dedicated Assurio support portal, support email, service hours and SLA are **not confirmed** by the public product page reviewed for this package.

## What to include

- Product environment and deployment type (without disclosing internal hostnames publicly).
- System ID, baseline ID and affected requirement, evidence or decision ID.
- User role, time and timezone, and the impacted workflow.
- Sanitized steps to reproduce and expected versus actual behavior.
- Whether the issue affects a critical assurance gate or permitted operating scope.
- Relevant correlation/event IDs and non-sensitive diagnostic attachments.

## What not to send through an unapproved channel

Passwords, tokens, API credentials, private customer data, unredacted model prompts containing secrets, sensitive telemetry or restricted evidence artifacts. Follow your organization's data-handling and incident-response policies.

## Escalation

If the issue suggests a safety-critical control failure or unauthorized data exposure, follow your organization's incident response and suspension procedures immediately; a support ticket should not replace containment.
