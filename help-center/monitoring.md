# Monitoring and reassessment

> Respond to change notifications and uncertain approval status

## What is a material change?

A change capable of affecting a requirement, control, risk or acceptance condition. Examples include a new model, altered tool permission, modified retrieval corpus, new user population, revised autonomy threshold or unexpected incident.

## Does every change require a full retest?

Not necessarily. Assurio's described approach links changes to the relevant requirements and evidence. A proportional review can revalidate affected claims while retaining unrelated evidence with a documented justification.

## Is an old approval still valid after a model update?

Do not assume so. Compare the approved baseline with the updated configuration and follow the declared impact-review procedure. If a critical claim or condition may have changed, the operating scope may need restriction until sufficient evidence is reviewed.

## I received an alert but cannot see the impact

Ask for the exact change event, old/new baseline identities, impacted requirement list and evidence records. Check authorization and integration health. Use your operational escalation route for safety-relevant incidents.
