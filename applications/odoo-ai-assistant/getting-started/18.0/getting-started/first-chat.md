# Odoo AI Assistant - Getting Started


<a id="step-1-install-the-module"></a>

## Step 1: Install the Module

Go to **Apps**, search for "AI Assistant", and click **Install**:

![Install AI Assistant](https://docs.sdlccorp.com/assets/images/generate-api-key-5f62c333c01cfcc9b3a175dda5dac771.webp)

<a id="step-2-open-ai-assistant"></a>

## Step 2: Open AI Assistant

Click **AI Assistant** in the Odoo sidebar. You'll see the welcome screen with example prompts:

![Welcome Screen](https://docs.sdlccorp.com/assets/images/welcome-screen-8382f291a3fae942514007198e76e02e.webp)

Try clicking any suggestion chip:

- "Show me all contacts"
- "Latest leads?"
- "Employees in sales"
- "Top sales this month"

<a id="step-3-select-an-ai-model"></a>

## Step 3: Select an AI Model

Choose your preferred AI model from the dropdown at the top:

![AI Models](https://docs.sdlccorp.com/assets/images/ai-models-1585d069445be2a4d79f671376fa6d94.webp)

| Model | Best For |
| --- | --- |
| **Llama 3.3 70B (Smart)** | Best accuracy. recommended for most queries |
| **Llama 3.1 8B (Fast)** | Fastest responses for simple questions |
| **DeepSeek R1 (Deep)** | Complex analytical queries |
| **Kimi K2 (Powerful)** | Advanced reasoning |
| **GPT O3 128k (Largest)** | Very large context queries |
| **GPT O3 20B** | Balanced performance |

tip

Start with **Llama 3.3 70B (Smart)**. it offers the best accuracy for business queries. You can switch models anytime without losing your conversation.

<a id="step-4-ask-your-question"></a>

## Step 4: Ask Your Question

Type your question in plain English and press **Enter**. The AI understands natural language including slang, abbreviations, and business terms.

![Understanding Response](https://docs.sdlccorp.com/assets/images/understanding-response-42b194ce85e4aa2bf740f6d8d49d5142.webp)

The AI returns:

- **Formatted text** answers for simple queries
- **Tables** for list-type data
- **Charts** for numerical summaries
- **Export buttons** for CSV/JSON download

<a id="example-queries"></a>

## Example Queries

**Contacts & CRM:**

- "Show me all contacts from India"
- "How many leads are in Won stage?"
- "What is the email of Mitchell Admin?"

**Sales & Invoices:**

- "What is the total sales amount?"
- "Which customer placed the highest order?"
- "List all unpaid invoices"

**HR & Employees:**

- "How many employees in each department?"
- "Who is the manager of Sales?"

**Products & Inventory:**

- "What is the most expensive product?"
- "Show me current stock levels"

caution

AI responses may not be 100% accurate. Please verify important business data before making decisions.
