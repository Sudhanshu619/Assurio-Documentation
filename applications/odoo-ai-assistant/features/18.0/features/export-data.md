# Export & Conversation History


<a id="why-export"></a>

## Why export

Keeping conversations only in the AI Assistant is fine for ad-hoc Q&A. Exporting is useful when you want to:

- Share a chat with a colleague who doesn't have access to the assistant.
- Pull the data the AI returned (CSV from a SELECT-style query) into a spreadsheet.
- Keep an archive of high-value chats for future reference.

The export format you pick should match the destination - JSON for re-import, CSV for spreadsheets, screenshot for documentation.

<a id="overview"></a>

## Overview

Every chat with the Odoo AI Assistant is saved so you can revisit it, share it, or pull the structured data the AI returned. This page covers the two formats you can export in and the history management features around them.

<a id="exporting-data"></a>

## Exporting Data

When the AI returns a table or list, you can export the results:

- **CSV**: spreadsheet-compatible format
- **JSON**: developer-friendly structured data
- **Copy to clipboard**: paste directly into other applications

<a id="downloading-conversations"></a>

## Downloading Conversations

Click the **Download Chat** button to save the entire conversation as a text file:

![Download Chat](https://docs.sdlccorp.com/assets/images/export-data-99dc2fcb6c37d35e7719b84358f89aca.webp)

This exports:

- All your questions
- All AI responses
- Tables and data included in responses

<a id="conversation-history"></a>

## Conversation History

Your past conversations are saved in the left sidebar:

![Conversation History](https://docs.sdlccorp.com/assets/images/conversations-5688f9c43ee16d6b10c69078b87bb78d.webp)

<a id="managing-conversations"></a>

### Managing Conversations

- **New Chat**: click the **"+ New Chat"** button to start a fresh conversation
- **History**: click any past conversation in the sidebar to reopen it
- **Search**: scroll through your conversation list to find previous queries

Each conversation is stored per-user: other users cannot see your chat history.
