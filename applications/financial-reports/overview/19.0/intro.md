# SDLC Financial Reports

A comprehensive dynamic financial reporting module for Odoo 17, 18, and 19 Community Edition that delivers 8 professional-grade reports with interactive views, PDF export, and Excel export capabilities -- all with zero configuration.

![Financial Reports Menu](https://docs.sdlccorp.com/assets/images/photo1-82219ec844d7df412620771ba1859bd6.webp)

<a id="what-is-sdlc-financial-reports"></a>

## What is SDLC Financial Reports?

SDLC Financial Reports extends Odoo's accounting module with a full suite of dynamic financial reports. Every report is generated automatically from your existing chart of accounts and journal entries -- no manual line setup or templates required. Each report offers an interactive on-screen view, one-click PDF generation, and Excel export with professional formatting.

<a id="8-financial-reports"></a>

## 8 Financial Reports

| Report | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **General Ledger** | Complete transaction history by account with running balance, initial balance, and account-level totals |
| **Partner Ledger** | Receivables and payables breakdown by partner with customer/supplier filtering and running balance |
| **Trial Balance** | Summary of all account balances with initial balance, debit, credit, ending balance, and comparison periods |
| **Aged Partner Balance** | Aging analysis with customizable buckets: Not Due, 0-30, 30-60, 60-90, 90-120, and 120+ days |
| **Profit & Loss** | Automatic income statement with income and expense sections, net profit calculation, and comparison periods |
| **Balance Sheet** | Assets, Liabilities, and Equity sections with sub-groupings and automatic total validation |
| **Cash Flow Statement** | Operating, Investing, and Financing activities with net cash change and detailed line items |
| **Analytic Summary** | Analysis by analytic accounts with debit, credit, and balance columns and plan-based grouping |

<a id="quick-start"></a>

## Quick Start

1. [Install the module](../../getting-started/19.0/getting-started/setup.md) -- Download and install `sdlc_financial_reports` from the Odoo Apps menu.
2. **Navigate to Reports** -- Go to **Accounting > Reporting > Financial Reports** to see all 8 reports.
3. **Select a Report** -- Click any report (e.g., General Ledger) to open the wizard.
4. **Set Filters** -- Choose your date range, accounts, and other options.
5. **View, Print, or Export** -- Click **View Report**, **Print PDF**, or **Export XLSX**.
