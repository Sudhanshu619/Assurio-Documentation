# Import Specific Products


<a id="when-to-use-targeted-product-import"></a>

## When to use targeted product import

Use this action when you only need one product (or a small set) refreshed:

- A specific product fell out of sync and needs to be repaired.
- You just published a new product in Shopify and want it in Odoo immediately, before the next scheduled sync.
- You're verifying the connector setup against a known reference product.

<a id="overview"></a>

## Overview

Targeted product import lets you pull a single Shopify product (or a small comma-separated list) into Odoo without re-running a full catalog import. It's the right tool when:

- A specific product fell out of sync and you need to repair it.
- You just published a new product in Shopify and want it in Odoo immediately.
- You're testing the connector and want to verify the round-trip on one item.

Import one or more specific products by their Shopify Product ID.

<a id="steps"></a>

## Steps

1. Navigate to **Shopify → Products → Import Specific Products**
2. Select your **Shopify Instance**
3. Enter one or more **Shopify Product IDs** (comma-separated)
4. Click **Import**

<a id="finding-shopify-product-ids"></a>

## Finding Shopify Product IDs

In Shopify Admin:

1. Go to **Products**
2. Click a product
3. The ID is in the URL: `shopify.com/admin/products/7890123456`
