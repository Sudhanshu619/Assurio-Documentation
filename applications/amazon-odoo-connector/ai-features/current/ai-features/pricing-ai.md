# Pricing & Profitability AI

Three AI features that optimize your pricing strategy and maximize profitability.

<a id="4-ai-price-optimizer"></a>

## 4. AI Price Optimizer

**Model:** `amazon.ai.pricing` | **Type:** Price Optimization

Suggests optimal pricing based on:

- Competitor analysis
- Buy Box price
- Cost price
- Margin rules

<a id="5-pricing-strategies"></a>

### 5 Pricing Strategies

| Strategy | Goal |
| --- | --- |
| **Competitive** | Match or slightly beat competitors |
| **Penetration** | Low price to gain market share |
| **Premium** | Higher price for quality positioning |
| **Value** | Balance between price and margin |
| **Undercut** | Aggressively below lowest competitor |

<a id="features"></a>

### Features

- **Confidence score** on each recommendation
- **AI reasoning** explaining the suggested price
- **Bulk reprice wizard** for multiple products at once

**Menu:** AI Tools > AI Pricing
**Bulk:** AI Tools > Bulk AI Reprice

<a id="how-to-use"></a>

### How to Use

1. Go to **AI Tools > AI Pricing**
2. Select the product(s) and strategy
3. Review AI suggestions with confidence scores
4. Apply the recommended price or edit manually
5. Push to Amazon

<a id="5-profit-calculator"></a>

## 5. Profit Calculator

**Model:** `amazon.profit.calculator` | **Type:** Financial Analysis

Calculates per-unit and 30-day net profit including:

- Amazon referral fee
- FBA fulfillment fee
- GST / VAT / sales tax
- Packaging costs
- Other costs you define

<a id="outputs"></a>

### Outputs

| Metric | What It Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Profit Margin %** | Net profit as percentage of revenue |
| **ROI %** | Return on cost investment |
| **Profitability Status** | See categories below |
| **AI Analysis** | Natural language explanation |

<a id="profitability-status"></a>

### Profitability Status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Highly Profitable** | Strong margins, healthy ROI |
| **Profitable** | Positive margins, acceptable returns |
| **Break Even** | Covering costs, minimal profit |
| **Loss Making** | Selling below cost - urgent action needed |

**Menu:** AI Tools > Profit Calculator

<a id="6-competitor-monitor"></a>

## 6. Competitor Monitor

**Model:** `amazon.competitor.monitor` | **Type:** Market Intelligence

Tracks competitor prices and market positioning. Alerts when:

- Competitors undercut your pricing
- You lose the Buy Box
- New competitors enter the listing

Provides data-driven insights for pricing strategy adjustments.

**Menu:** AI Tools > Competitor Monitor

<a id="use-cases"></a>

### Use Cases

- **Dynamic pricing** - React to competitor price changes in real-time
- **Buy Box recovery** - Know when to adjust to win back the Buy Box
- **Market intelligence** - Track competitor count over time
- **Hijack detection** - Spot unauthorized sellers on your listing

tip

Combine **Profit Calculator** with **AI Price Optimizer** for intelligent repricing. The Profit Calculator sets your minimum viable price (break-even point), and the Price Optimizer finds the highest price above that threshold that stays competitive.
